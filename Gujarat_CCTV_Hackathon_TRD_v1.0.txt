GUJARAT POLICE INNOVATION CHALLENGE 2026
TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (TRD)
Statewide CCTV Federation, Unified Viewing, Selective AI Analytics & Investigation Platform
TRD v1.0 — Developer-Ready Build Specification

==============================================================================

1. Document Purpose
-------------------
• This TRD converts the product/architecture requirements into implementation-ready technical requirements for a prototype that can be demonstrated on government-style sandbox feeds and extended toward statewide deployment.
• The system shall integrate heterogeneous departmental CCTV/VMS environments without requiring immediate replacement of existing departmental systems. It shall provide camera registry/GIS, feed federation, unified viewing, selective AI/ANPR, event streaming, vehicle search, cross-camera route reconstruction, watchlist correlation, alerts, evidence, audit and operational health monitoring.

2. Product Scope
----------------
• IN SCOPE: camera registry and GIS; departmental/VMS federation; RTSP/WebRTC/HLS ingestion; stream health; unified live viewing; selective edge/regional AI; vehicle detection and ANPR; plate normalization; event bus; searchable observations; cross-camera vehicle correlation; watchlist adapters; real-time alerts; investigation timeline/map; evidence export; RBAC; audit; observability; load testing.
• OUT OF SCOPE FOR MVP: replacement of every departmental VMS; unconditional ingestion of all statewide video into one central storage cluster; direct database access to government systems without authorized interfaces; unrestricted private-camera access; fully autonomous enforcement decisions; claims of production 80,000-camera capacity without measured testing.

3. Architecture Requirements
----------------------------
• ARCH-001: Model 1 — Central CCTV Registry & GIS Foundation — shall be implemented as a mandatory foundation.
• ARCH-002: Model 3 — Federation/Middleware — shall provide adapters between central services and heterogeneous departmental VMS/cameras.
• ARCH-003: Model 2 — Unified Viewing — shall expose authorized live streams through a common operator experience.
• ARCH-004: Selective AI shall run at edge/regional GPU workers where practical instead of forcing every frame through one central AI cluster.
• ARCH-005: Video transport and intelligence events shall be decoupled. Raw video is high-bandwidth; normalized events/metadata shall flow through an event platform.
• ARCH-006: The system shall be horizontally scalable by adding gateways, AI workers, brokers, API instances and search/storage capacity.
• ARCH-007: All external integrations shall use adapters/contracts so a vendor or government API can be replaced without rewriting core services.

5. Core Services
----------------
• 5.1 Camera Registry Service — authoritative metadata for cameras, departments, sites, streams, VMS, locations, health, analytics profiles and access policy.
• 5.2 GIS Service — camera map, status layers, alert layers and investigation routes.
• 5.3 Federation Service — connector lifecycle, VMS discovery/registration, stream URLs, protocol adapters and vendor-specific integration.
• 5.4 Stream Gateway — protocol normalization, reconnect, buffering, stream health and viewer session management.
• 5.5 AI Orchestrator — assigns analytics profiles to cameras/workers, controls sampling and records model/version metadata.
• 5.6 ANPR/Vehicle Service — detection, OCR, normalization, confidence scoring and observation creation.
• 5.7 Event Service — durable event publication, consumption, deduplication and schema validation.
• 5.8 Search Service — plate/time/camera/district/event queries and indexed observations.
• 5.9 Correlation Service — cross-camera route reconstruction using time, geography, direction and confidence constraints.
• 5.10 Watchlist Gateway — normalized interface for authorized VAHAN/SARATHI/eGujCop/CCTNS/AFIS/NAFIS integrations.
• 5.11 Alert Service — rule evaluation, severity, assignment, acknowledgement, escalation and closure.
• 5.12 Evidence Service — snapshots/clips, hashes, manifests, export and access logs.
• 5.13 Identity/RBAC Service — users, roles, scopes, tokens and permission enforcement.
• 5.14 Audit Service — immutable security and operational audit events.
• 5.15 Observability Service — metrics, logs, traces, health and capacity dashboards.

6. Functional Requirements
--------------------------
• FR-001 Camera onboarding: authorized administrators shall create, update, disable and validate camera records.
• FR-002 GIS: every active camera shall be geolocated and visible on the map when coordinates are available.
• FR-003 Stream validation: the platform shall test connectivity before a camera is marked operational.
• FR-004 Protocol support: ingestion architecture shall support RTSP, WebRTC and HLS through a common internal stream abstraction.
• FR-005 Reconnect: temporary camera/network failure shall trigger bounded retries and health-state transitions.
• FR-006 Unified viewing: authorized users shall open one or more live feeds without needing to understand the source VMS vendor.
• FR-007 AI profiles: administrators shall enable/disable analytics per camera and select a profile.
• FR-008 Vehicle detection: AI shall create vehicle observations with timestamp, camera, location and confidence.
• FR-009 ANPR: OCR shall produce raw and normalized plate values and separate detection/OCR confidence.
• FR-010 Watchlist match: authorized watchlist queries shall generate possible-match events rather than asserting guilt/identity.
• FR-011 Cross-camera search: users shall search a target plate across cameras and time ranges.
• FR-012 Route reconstruction: observations shall be ordered and filtered for temporal/geographic plausibility; uncertain gaps shall be shown.
• FR-013 Alerts: configurable rules shall create alerts with severity, reason, confidence and evidence.
• FR-014 Investigation: users shall view a timeline, map, camera details and supporting evidence.
• FR-015 Evidence: authorized users shall export evidence with metadata and SHA-256 hash.
• FR-016 Audit: all sensitive searches, views, exports, role changes and alert actions shall be logged.
• FR-017 Health: operators shall see camera, stream, AI, event-bus, API and storage health.
• FR-018 Reporting: the platform shall export machine-readable JSON/CSV and human-readable incident reports.

7. Non-Functional Requirements
------------------------------
• NFR-001 Availability: individual camera or worker failure shall not crash the central platform.
• NFR-002 Scalability: services shall support horizontal replication and stateless API scaling where possible.
• NFR-003 Performance: target alert latency should be measured end-to-end from frame/event ingestion to UI notification; final SLA values shall be set after benchmark testing.
• NFR-004 Security: TLS for network traffic, encryption at rest where supported, RBAC, least privilege, secrets management and audit logging are mandatory.
• NFR-005 Reliability: event processing shall be retryable and idempotent.
• NFR-006 Observability: all critical services shall expose health, metrics and structured logs.
• NFR-007 Maintainability: code shall use modular services, typed contracts, versioned APIs and automated tests.
• NFR-008 Portability: deployment shall be containerized and reproducible.
• NFR-009 Data governance: retention and access policy shall be configurable by source/department and enforced automatically.
• NFR-010 Evidence integrity: exported evidence shall have a cryptographic hash and chain-of-custody metadata.

9. API Requirements
-------------------
• API style: REST/JSON for control-plane and search operations; WebSocket/SSE for live alert/event updates; streaming protocols remain in the media plane.
• All APIs shall be versioned under /api/v1.
• Every request shall support a correlation/request ID.
• Authentication shall use short-lived access tokens or approved SSO integration.
• Authorization shall be checked server-side for every sensitive resource.
• Pagination is mandatory for list/search endpoints.
• Errors shall use a consistent schema: code, message, request_id, details.

12. UI/UX Technical Requirements
--------------------------------
• Screen 1 — Command Dashboard: alerts, camera health, active feeds, map and system health.
• Screen 2 — Camera Registry/GIS: searchable camera table + map; filters for department, district, status and analytics profile.
• Screen 3 — Unified Viewer: grid of authorized streams; camera metadata and health visible.
• Screen 4 — Vehicle Investigation: plate search, timeline, route map, observations and evidence.
• Screen 5 — Alert Detail: alert reason, confidence, source camera, evidence, watchlist status and operator actions.
• Screen 6 — Evidence/Report: preview, metadata, hash, export and audit history.
• Screen 7 — Administration: users, roles, camera onboarding, analytics profiles, integrations and policies.
• UX rule: the main judge workflow — enter plate -> see sightings -> see route -> open evidence -> generate report — should be executable in a few clicks.

13. AI/ANPR Technical Requirements
----------------------------------
• Use a pluggable model interface so detector/OCR models can be changed without changing business logic.
• Every inference must record model name/version, threshold and timestamp.
• Store plate_raw and plate_normalized separately.
• Normalize uppercase, whitespace, punctuation and known OCR-confusion characters only according to a controlled rule set.
• Do not convert uncertain OCR into a definite identity.
• Alert thresholds shall be configurable and benchmarked on representative data.
• AI workers shall expose queue depth, inference latency, FPS, GPU utilization and error metrics.

14. Cross-Camera Correlation
----------------------------
• Inputs: plate similarity, timestamp, camera coordinates, direction, vehicle attributes and confidence.
• Reject impossible transitions using configurable minimum/maximum travel-time constraints.
• Deduplicate near-identical observations.
• Produce route confidence and explicit uncertainty/gaps.
• Never fabricate an intermediate camera observation.
• Store the observations that caused each route link so an investigator can explain the result.

15. Security Requirements
-------------------------
• SEC-001 MFA/SSO shall be supported where available.
• SEC-002 RBAC shall be scoped by role, department, district, camera group and sensitive operation.
• SEC-003 No camera endpoint shall be publicly exposed by default.
• SEC-004 Secrets shall not be stored in source code or Git.
• SEC-005 TLS shall protect control-plane and integration traffic.
• SEC-006 Sensitive searches and exports shall be audited.
• SEC-007 Evidence exports shall be hashed.
• SEC-008 API rate limiting and input validation shall be implemented.
• SEC-009 Dependencies and containers shall be vulnerability-scanned.
• SEC-010 Government credentials/data used for testing shall never be committed to the repository.

16. Recommended Technology Stack
--------------------------------
• Frontend: React + TypeScript + Vite/Next.js; MapLibre GL JS or Leaflet; WebSocket/SSE client.
• Backend: Python FastAPI for AI/search/integration services and/or Node.js/NestJS for control-plane services. Use one primary backend language for the prototype unless a separate AI service is justified.
• Database: PostgreSQL + PostGIS for authoritative metadata and GIS.
• Search: OpenSearch/Elasticsearch for high-volume observation search if PostgreSQL indexing becomes insufficient.
• Event bus: Apache Kafka; Redis may be used for cache/session/short-lived state.
• Media: FFmpeg/GStreamer plus protocol-specific gateway components; WebRTC gateway as required.
• AI: Python, PyTorch/ONNX Runtime/TensorRT where supported, GPU workers.
• Object storage: S3-compatible storage such as MinIO for prototype; production may use approved government/object storage.
• Deployment: Docker Compose for single-node demo; Kubernetes for production-style regional/state deployment.
• Observability: Prometheus + Grafana + OpenTelemetry; centralized structured logs.
• CI/CD: GitHub Actions/GitLab CI/Jenkins according to project environment.

18. Deployment Requirements
---------------------------
• Prototype: Docker Compose on a high-spec development machine or small server. Use simulated feeds and local object storage.
• Scale test: Kubernetes with multiple API pods, event-bus brokers, AI workers and storage/search replicas.
• Regional production concept: camera/VMS -> regional gateway -> regional AI -> state event platform.
• Use infrastructure-as-code where feasible.
• All deployments must have health checks, readiness probes, liveness probes and resource limits.
• Configuration must be environment-based; secrets must be injected through a secure mechanism.

19. Performance & Scale Test Plan
---------------------------------
• Test 10, 30, 50 and 100 simultaneous feeds in the prototype/benchmark environment.
• Measure ingest success, stream latency, ANPR latency, event throughput, alert latency, route-reconstruction latency, CPU/GPU/RAM, network bandwidth, queue lag and database/search latency.
• Run failure tests: camera disconnect/reconnect storm, AI worker crash, event-bus disruption, database failover and temporary network loss.
• Do not claim 80,000-camera support from a small demo. Publish measured benchmark data and an extrapolated capacity model.
• Capacity equations: ingress bandwidth = cameras × average bitrate; AI load = cameras × sampled FPS × model cost; event rate = cameras × average event rate; storage = bitrate × cameras × retention.

20. Testing Strategy
--------------------
• Unit tests: plate normalization, confidence logic, RBAC, route constraints, event validation.
• Integration tests: camera registration, stream validation, event bus, search, watchlist adapter, evidence export.
• E2E test: feed -> AI -> observation -> search -> route -> alert -> evidence -> audit.
• Security tests: authentication bypass, authorization bypass, injection, XSS, unsafe file upload, secret leakage and insecure direct object references.
• Resilience tests: reconnect, retry, duplicate event, worker failure and delayed event delivery.
• AI tests: day/night, low resolution, multiple vehicle classes, plate angle/occlusion and false-positive measurement.

23. Risks & Mitigations
-----------------------
• Vendor incompatibility -> adapter/plugin architecture; validate ONVIF/RTSP/vendor APIs early.
• Poor government-feed compatibility -> build protocol adapters and a feed simulator for every supported protocol.
• ANPR false positives -> confidence thresholds, normalization, evidence and human review.
• Network bandwidth bottleneck -> regional gateways, selective AI, metadata-first architecture.
• Central bottleneck -> Kafka, horizontal workers, stateless APIs and regional processing.
• Security failure -> threat model, RBAC, MFA, TLS, secrets management, audit and security tests.
• Overclaiming scale -> benchmark and publish measured limits.
• Demo failure -> deterministic replayable test scenario plus live/simulated feed fallback.
• Government API unavailable -> interface adapter + mock contract; never claim real access without authorization.
